Privacy Policy

Application: “N8n workflows” · Last updated: 27 August 2026

This application supports private, internal workflow automation built with n8n. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Google OAuth and Google services

  • The application uses Google OAuth to access authorized Google services.
  • Google user data is used only to operate the workflows configured by the account owner.
  • Gmail access may be used to send, read, classify, or process email when required by a configured workflow.

How data is used

  • Data is not sold.
  • Data is not used for advertising.
  • Data is not shared with unrelated third parties.

Tokens and storage

OAuth tokens are stored by n8n in its credential system and are not intentionally exposed in public files.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the Google credential in n8n, or through the Google Account permissions page.

Intended use

The application is intended for private/internal automation use. It is not a public SaaS product and is not offered to external customers.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about data handling can be sent to karen.plata.ai.work@gmail.com.
